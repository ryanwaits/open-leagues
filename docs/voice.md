# Voice

How open-leagues writes, on the landing page, in the docs, in a skill, in an
error. Distilled on 2026-09-02 from Mailchimp, GOV.UK, Google and Microsoft
style guides, Orwell, Strunk, Basecamp, and the heroes of about thirty live
developer-tool and MCP-server pages.

open-leagues writes like a clerk filing a box score. Every sentence names a
thing you can fetch (a Sleeper league by id, a player's week, a closing line,
a graded hypothesis), the span it covers, and where it came from. It reports
counts, dates, and sources, and stops. It never praises itself, never ranks a
player, never picks a side, never places a bet, and says so in one plain
sentence when the reader needs the boundary.

## Rules

1. Front-load the thing the reader came for.
2. Sentences under 25 words. Paragraphs under five sentences.
3. Name the concrete thing, with a number or a date. "Closing lines for every
   game since 1999", not "deep historical coverage".
4. Cut any word that can go.
5. Active voice, short verbs: reads, returns, grades, lists.
6. State facts positively. Negate only when the negation is the fact ("it does
   not place bets").
7. No metaphor or figure of speech you have seen in print.
8. No "easy", "simply", "just".
9. No "please", no exclamation marks, no rhetorical questions.
10. Read it aloud. If you would not say it to a friend, rewrite it.
11. No evaluative adjectives, no "every" or "most" success claims.
12. No triads, no "not X, it's Y", no "X. Y." fragment slogans, no em dashes,
    no winks. Sentence-case headings.

## Blocklist

seamless, robust, leverage, powerful, unlock, elevate, empower, streamline,
harness, effortless, easy, easily, simply, just, cutting-edge, best-in-class,
world-class, comprehensive, intelligent, smart, supercharge, game-changer,
transform, delve, landscape, ecosystem, pivotal, key (adjective), enable,
allows you to, in order to, please.

Substitutes: use; lets you; to; reads, returns, lists; the number itself; the
source name; the timestamp; "open source"; "no account"; "does not" for a
boundary.

## Hero shape

H1 of three to nine words that names the category and the audience. Sub of
under 50 words that inventories what it returns, states the guarantee
(timestamp and source), and draws the boundary. Install command first, then a
handful of quoted example prompts. Nothing else above the fold.
